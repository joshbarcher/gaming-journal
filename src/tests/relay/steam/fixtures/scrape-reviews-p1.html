Went fishing for guns. Would do it again. 10/10.

This game was not what I was expecting. The changes from Borderlands 2 to 3 were small and 50% of the game was just more BL2. This on the other hand is a complete reimagining of the series. So many things were just altered for the better or new things added, including:

- Huge open world, no more loading screens
- Exploration has been emphasized
- Puzzles in the world (not for everyone, but I like them)
- Jumping puzzles!
- Large refactoring of guns, their components, cooldowns with heavy weapons & grenades
- Double jumping and gliding!
- Grapple hook!
- They finally mixed up the mobs and made them really feel fresh. Unlike from Borderlands 2 to 3
- A new engine with advanced lighting, draw distances, and textures. It looks beautiful and next-gen, but didn't lose the Borderlands feel.
- Vendor redesign
- New types of quests
- New shield type (called an armor shield)
- ... and more ...

It's sad that at launch so many people are having issues running it. Underneath the performance problems is an amazing game. I was able to get great performance with a 4090 and 4k/DLSS-quality/framegen/RTSS with 100-120fps.

Wow, just wow!

This runs in the background while playing a game and can scale the resolution or generate frames using AI. This is great for a few specific cases:

- playing old games capped at 30fps (eg. FFX remaster)
- playing new games that are still capped for some reason (eg. Dave the diver)
- increasing fps on lower end hardware

Running the mediocre FFX port at 90 fps made me instantly fall in love with this tool. It even increases the frames for prerendered cutscenes, interpolated new frames in the videos. I can now replay this classic without my eyes falling out of my head.

I also tested Dave the Diver at 120 fps on my Rog Ally. It is silky smooth at 1080p on handheld.

There are some caveats (input lag, fixed framerate in game is required). But this technique is fantastic as it mostly exists outside of the game and gpu-vendor software. I'm really excited to see what they add to this program, or others like it.
